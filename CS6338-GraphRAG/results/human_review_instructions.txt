HUMAN REVIEW RUBRIC

Review each saved answer alongside the question, reference answer, retrieved text,
and graph facts. Reference labels are silver; note a disputed label rather than
forcing agreement with it. Do not copy local-judge scores into human rating fields.

Factuality (1-5)
1: The main answer is incorrect or unsupported.
2: Major factual errors remain despite some correct details.
3: A mixture of supported and unsupported claims.
4: The main answer is supported, with a minor factual error or qualification missing.
5: All substantive claims are supported and appropriately qualified.

Completeness (1-5)
1: Does not address the question or required path.
2: Omits most required links or evidence.
3: Addresses part of the answer but leaves an important gap.
4: Covers the main answer and path with a minor omission.
5: Covers all required relationships, provenance, and uncertainty.

Coherence (1-5)
1: Unreadable, contradictory, or unrelated.
2: Difficult to follow with major organization problems.
3: Understandable but unclear in places.
4: Clear, with a minor wording or organization issue.
5: Clear, concise, logically connected, and easy to verify.

An appropriate abstention can be factually sound even if it does not provide the
reference answer. Judge completeness against the evidence actually available and
note missing retrieval separately. A citation ID's existence is not sufficient:
read its text and check whether it supports the corresponding assertion.

Write short notes for scores below 4 and for any suspected reference-label problem.
Do not claim a human evaluation has occurred until a person fills out the worksheet.
